# Glossary

## Model

A distinct FOSLN watch design, sold under its own name. **ACIER** is a Model.

_Avoid_: style, line.

## Dial

The face a Model is offered in. Choosing a Dial is how a customer picks between versions of one Model. ACIER comes in two Dials, **Chrome** and **Onyx**.

_Avoid_: colourway, colour, finish.
