# Glossary

## Model

A distinct FOSLN watch design, sold under its own name. The launch Models are **ACIER**, with a round case, and **CADRE**, with a square case.

_Avoid_: style, line.

## Dial

The face a Model is offered in. Choosing a Dial is how a customer picks between versions of one Model. ACIER comes in two Dials, **Chrome** and **Onyx**. CADRE comes in two Dials, one black and one champagne; their names are not yet chosen.

_Avoid_: colourway, colour, finish.

## Supplier

The company that holds FOSLN's stock and sends each order straight to the customer. FOSLN never holds stock itself.

_Avoid_: vendor, warehouse, 3PL.

## Dispatch

The moment the Supplier hands an order to the carrier. Dispatch is not delivery: "dispatched within 24 hours" says nothing about when the order arrives.

_Avoid_: ship, fulfil (both blur dispatch and delivery).

## Market

A country FOSLN sells into, with its own tax, delivery and consumer rules. A Market's prices can be in a currency other than its own. The launch Market is **Australia**, priced in USD.

_Avoid_: region, territory.

## Regular price

The one price a Model is sold at, the same for every Dial. A Model has one regular price at a time. A change of price makes a new regular price.

_Avoid_: launch price, introductory price, RRP, compare-at price.

## Was price

A former regular price shown struck through next to a lower sale price. It is honest only if the Model really sold at that regular price for a reasonable period just before the sale.

_Avoid_: compare-at price, original price, retail value.

## Sale

A period when a Model sells below its regular price, with the regular price shown as the was price. An offer that states a saving against the regular price, such as "20% off", is a sale.

_Avoid_: promo, deal, discount.

## Offer

A short-term incentive that leaves the shown price unchanged, such as free express delivery, a gift or store credit. An offer that states a saving is a sale, not an offer.

_Avoid_: promo, deal, discount.
