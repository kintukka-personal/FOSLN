# 2. No sale at launch

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 9 October 2026
- **Decided in:** [Decide the launch price for each Model](https://github.com/Kintukka2/FOSLN-WATCH/issues/28)

## Context

Both reference designs show a sale price: $97, with $225 or $249 struck through. Neither Model has ever been offered at any price.

FOSLN's only launch Market is Australia, so the Australian Consumer Law applies. The ACCC's test for a was price is whether consumers would really have paid the regular price for a reasonable period just before the sale.

Comparable direct-to-consumer brands, such as MVMT, Vincero and the Australian brand Christian Paul, are on sale almost all the time. The ACCC has taken action against this pattern:

- **Kogan** was ordered to pay A$350,000 in 2020.
- **Dell Australia** was ordered to pay A$10 million in 2023.
- **Coles:** in May 2026, the Federal Court found most of the sampled "Down Down" tickets misleading.

## Decision

Each Model launches at one regular price, with no sale and no was price.

- **ACIER** is **$149** and **CADRE** is **$169**, in USD, the same for every Dial.
- No launch price will rise later.
- A sale is possible only after the regular price has held for a reasonable period. [Decide how prices are presented](https://github.com/Kintukka2/FOSLN-WATCH/issues/24) sets the rules for it.
- A later change of price makes a new regular price. It never becomes a was price.

## Alternatives considered

- **Sell at $97 from the first day, with a higher price struck through.** This is the reference designs' pattern. It was rejected because no was price exists on launch day, so the comparison would be misleading.
- **Sell at the regular price first, then run frequent sales at $97.** This was rejected because a sale that runs most of the time becomes the real price. The struck-through price then becomes misleading, which is the pattern behind the cases above.
- **A lower regular price of $97.** This was rejected because it puts FOSLN below its intended premium-fashion position. Comparable brands sell at about US$150–200.

## Consequences

- **The designs must change.** The reference designs' price blocks must show one plain price. They must not reuse the $97, $225 or $249 figures.
- **No urgency from a discount at launch.** Launch marketing can't use a discount to create urgency. Offers without a was price, such as free 4-day delivery, are still open in "Decide how prices are presented".
- **A later sale needs a record of real prices.** FOSLN must keep evidence of each regular price and the dates it applied.
- **The prices clear the margin floor** with GST from the first day. The floor is 55% of the price and US$50 for each order. The prices stay the same when FOSLN registers for GST.
